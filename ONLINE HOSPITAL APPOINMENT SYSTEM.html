Online Appointment Booking System

System Overview

In an effort to streamline the appointment process and enhance patient experience, MedCare Hospital has developed an online appointment booking system. This platform allows patients to book appointments with doctors from various specialties at their convenience. Key functionalities include user registration and login, browsing available doctors and appointment slots, booking and canceling appointments, and receiving appointment reminders via email or SMS.

Patients can also view their appointment history and update their personal details. The system ensures data security and compliance with healthcare regulations to protect patient information. This online service aims to reduce wait times, minimize paperwork, and provide a seamless experience for patients and healthcare providers alike. This website helps the patient to search any hospital and book appointments as they prefer.

Patient Features & Modules
